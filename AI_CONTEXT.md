# AI_CONTEXT: how O'Deadly Skyrim works

This file is for AI assistants, bots and modders who want to understand this mod quickly and extend it (new vanilla-environment scenarios, patches, SkyrimNet hooks). Everything written by That Guy (Coronerra) is MIT. Third-party APIs belong to their authors (see LICENSE).

## 1. What problem it solves

Most "dangerous world" adult mods for Skyrim are narrow (nights only, prisons only) and often treat the player as the only victim. O'Deadly Skyrim is one all-in-one, toggleable engine for OStim + optional SkyrimNet. Events already in vanilla Skyrim (sleep, wait, lingering in a jail or barracks, inns, defeat, trespass, roads, wilderness) can start a scene with the people who are actually there, including followers. The end user turns on what they want. A master kill switch and a panic hotkey exist for family-safe play.

It was based on the idea of [ODangerous Nights](https://www.patreon.com/posts/166577223). The author never downloaded it, so compatibility is unknown, and it is not required — this already does the same thing and more. No ODN files are used; this is not an ODN patch. If `ODangerousNights.esp` is present, the engine tries to stay out of the way after an ODN attack; feedback on that path is welcome.

## 2. Hard rules

- Adults only. Children are refused for every role. There is no toggle.
- The master switch defaults to OFF. With it off, nothing in the mod can fire.
- The panic key (unbound by default) or turning the master off aborts scenes, deletes spawns, returns stolen gold, clears calming / essential flags, purges pending SkyrimNet dialogue, cancels timers, and leaves the switch OFF. In 0.2 it also cancels pending morning-after and bathing rolls, removes a spawned watcher and the easter-egg rabbits / butterflies, and switches the drink / wash listeners off.
- Every module is its own MCM toggle. Every 0.2 module defaults to OFF. The 0.3 follower rescue defaults to OFF. The 0.3 SkyrimNet bridges default ON but only do anything when their mod is installed, and send nothing with the master off. The 0.4 climax-fauna menu defaults to Off (`iFaunaMode` 0). The 0.5 Baka Defeat bridge defaults ON but only does anything when `SexLabDefeat.esp` is installed, and sends nothing with the master off. The 0.9 Savior Complex and Killer Rabbit vs Vigilants options default to OFF (`bSavior`, `bBunnyFight`; 2% chances). Every 1.0 option defaults to OFF (`bJoinIn`, `bCrWild`, `bWolfNight`, `bBeastOffer`).
- Scenarios are based on the standard Skyrim environment. Optional mods (SkyrimNet, OCreatures, OCum, ZaZ, OStimNet, ODN, and in 0.2 iActions, Dirt and Blood, Bathing in Skyrim, Blood and OStim, Sacrosanct, JContainers; in 0.3 OStim Kidnapped Redux and ODefeat Reforged; in 0.4 Mihail's The Killer Rabbit of Caerbannog, `mihailkillerbunny.esp`; in 0.5 Baka Defeat – OStim Edition, `SexLabDefeat.esp`; in 1.0 OStim Beast Form, `OStim_Beast_Form_Merged.esp` or `OStim_Beast_Form_TWO.esp`) are soft-detected at runtime and are never plugin masters. Code that needs another mod's script types sits in small interface scripts (`ODS_DnB`, `ODS_IActions`, `ODS_Vamp`) whose global functions are only called after the plugin / SKSE plugin is confirmed loaded. Compile-only stubs for those types live in `tools/stubs/` and are never shipped.

## 4. Engine

Event-based, no polling. One event at a time, a 4-hour cooldown (default), and an abort check at every step. Triggers: sleep (start and stop), the sleep/wait menu, location change, bleedout, OStim thread start/end/scene-changed and `ostim_actor_orgasm`, the panic key, and one-shot timers (jail linger, road travel). 0.2 adds, only while the related module is on: `OnObjectEquipped` (potions) on the player alias, `OnMagicEffectApply` on the player and follower aliases (held in a "Watching" alias state so the handlers do not exist otherwise), and the Bathing in Skyrim mod events. 0.9 Savior Complex and Killer Rabbit vs Vigilants share that same one-shot travel timer (plus wait rolls); a 1 s `RegisterForSingleUpdate` is used only while a rescue fight or the rabbit fight is already running, then it drops back to the existing cleanup timer. 1.0 wild creature and werewolf events share the same travel timer. The 1.0 beast-form offer uses `ODS_PlayerAlias.OnRaceSwitchComplete` and then a bounded single-update chain on the player alias (first check 12 s, retries 10 s while busy or in combat, at most 12, one offer per transformation, cleared on load).

Location categories (walled-city player home, other player home, inn, city/town, settlement, wilderness, camp, dungeon, plus jail/barracks) are resolved from vanilla location keywords and cell/location names. If SkyrimNet beta 29+ is present and "Use SkyrimNet places" is on (default), a tagged named place wins over keywords. Untagged places fall back to keywords. The AI is not asked to classify untagged cells.

## 5. Modules

Night attacks (followers by default), cheating spouse, caught in the act, follower betrayal, imprisonment and barracks in every hold (sleep, wait or linger — never on door entry), inns (drunk patrons, spiked drinks), defeat (bleedout; optional "keep the player alive"), trespass, travel and roads (gold is moved to the thief, never deleted), optional OCreatures types probed against installed scenes, vampire seduction (present vampires only, no spawns), blackmail and public rumours (SkyrimNet), OCum / ZaZ options, group scenes with fallback to one-on-one, drunk/skooma chance multiplier, named/essential NPC toggle (default off).

Defaults: everything off except the night-attack module inside the master switch, and the drunk/skooma modifier. Player-as-victim is off in most modules.

### 0.3 Follower rescue (mid-battle; `bRescue`, OFF by default)

Side thread, not a staged encounter: it never sets `bBusy`, never calls `StageDefeat`, `Pacify`, `CalmFollowers` or `AddSecondVictim`, and never puts the player in the scene.

- Trigger: `ODS_FollowerAlias.OnEnterBleedout` → `OnActorBleedout` → `TryFollowerRescue` before the Defeat path. Conditions: `Ready()`, `bRescue`, no rescue running, not `bBusy`; follower (`IsFollowerActor`), adult, not on the protect list, guard rule, local gender filter `iRescueGender` (0 both, 1 female, 2 male; `iVictimGender` untouched); player alive, not in bleedout, not in OStim, not held by Kidnapped Redux / ODefeat Reforged; 1 game hour since the last rescue; `fRescueChance` (50). `FillFollowerAliases` also fills the follower aliases while `bRescue` is on.
- Attacker: first actor within 1500 units of the follower who is not a follower, not in bleedout, hostile to the player or follower, `BaseEligible` (adult, `NamedOK`), `CreatureAllowed`, guard rule, and has an aggressive scene with the follower (`SceneForSet`, forced).
- Calming only the pair: attacker Aggression 0 (old value saved), `StopCombat`, `StopCombatAlarm`, added to `PlayerFaction` if not already in it (so followers stop attacking it); not ghosted. Follower: `StopCombat`, health to 50%, `ActorBase.SetProtected(true)` for the scene only if it was neither essential nor protected. One bounded `Wait(1.0)`, then `OThreadBuilder` with `NoPlayerControl`, `NoFurniture`, `UndressActors`, the normal duration range and metadata `odangerous,aggressive,follower_rescue`.
- The attacker goes into `Extra3Alias` (`ODS_AttackerAlias`, only used by 4-attacker group encounters, which cannot start while a rescue runs). `ODS_AttackerAlias` asks `IsRescueAlias(self)`: player `OnHit` / `OnActivate` → `RescueOutcome(1)` (rescued), `OnDeath` → `RescueOutcome(2)`; otherwise the old `Intervene()`.
- `ostim_thread_end` for the rescue thread id → `RescueOutcome(0)` (not in time). The player going into bleedout during a rescue also counts as not in time, then the normal player path runs. `RescueRelease` stops the thread, clears the alias, restores faction and aggression, sends the attacker back into combat with the player, removes the temporary protected flag and gives the follower 50% health. Panic / master off (`AbortAll`) and game load release without SkyrimNet text.
- While it runs: `OtherBusy()` is true (O'Deadly encounters wait), another downed follower bleeds out normally, and `OnOStimThreadStart` skips the caught / watched checks.

### 0.6 startup fix (`ODS_Main`, `ODS_MCM`; no new records)

- The protected-NPC list property is `ProtectedForms` (`IsProtected`, `ToggleProtected`, MCM `PageProtected`). The 0.5 property was dropped and is never read; on update that list starts empty.
- `InitArrays` creates the runtime arrays first (`kAtt`, `kVic`, `kSpawned`, `fOldAgg`, `fOldConf`, `bAddedFac`, `kPacified`, `fPacAgg`, `bCalmed`, `bCrType`, `iCrStatus`, `fDrinkTimes`, `kFauna`, `kBathFx`, the `iBaka*` / `kBaka*` caches), then the settings arrays (`ProtectedForms`, `OvrLoc`, `OvrAtk`, `OvrCheat`, `OvrJail`, `PlaceNames`, `PlaceCats`), each checked on its own, then `MigrateFaunaMode()`.
- Never write `arr == None`, `arr != None` or `arr = None` for an array: Caprica compiles the `None` literal to a cast into an array-typed temp, which the Skyrim VM rejects ("Cannot cast from None to Form[]"). Test with `If !arr`, build through a local (`Form[] t = new Form[64]` then `arr = t`), and clear with a never-assigned script variable of the same type (`kNoActors`, `kNoForms`, MCM `oNoInts`).
- Scene actor lists come from `NewActorArray(n)` (literal `new Actor[1..8]`, exact length); `OActorUtil.CreateArray` is not used. `OActorUtil.Sort`, `ToArray` and `GetActorsInRangeV2` still are. MCM `GetVersion` 7. SkyrimNet pack 0.6.0.

### 0.7 rest timing and bath timing (`ODS_Main` only; no new records)

- Nothing is moved, spawned or staged while a sleep or wait is running or the Sleep/Wait Menu is open. Other mods track that window: NPC AI Position Fix sinks `TESMoveAttachDetachEvent` from sleep/wait start until the menu closes and repositions those actors on stop, so actors we spawned and later deleted there caused a crash on the next save.
- `OnSleepStart` only rolls (`MorningCheck`, then `HandleRest` -> `RollKinds`) and stores `bRestPending` / `iRestKind` / `iRestEpoch` with `bBusy` held. `OnSleepStop(abInterrupted)` -> `RunPendingRest`, with `OnMenuClose` (when `bSleptThisMenu`) as a fallback; flags are cleared before any wait, so it runs once. If the sleep was interrupted, or `Alive(iRestEpoch)` fails, the event is dropped and `bBusy` freed. `RestSettle` = bounded `WaitMenuMode` loop until the menu closes (`REST_MENU_CAP` 10 s), then `Utility.Wait(REST_SETTLE)` 1 s, before `StageWithFade(kind, ep, true)`. Waits do `RestSettle` too. The morning after uses the same path.
- `StageWithFade(fromRest)` waits up to 5 s for `GetSitState() == 0`, and only calls `PlayerRef.MoveTo(PlayerRef)` if the player is still in the bed.
- Pending rest state is cleared by `OnGameLoaded`, `AbortAll`, and at the next `OnSleepStart` if stale.
- Bath: `OnBathStart` waits while `StillBathing` up to `BATH_WAIT_CAP` 150 s (skips the scene if still bathing), then `BATH_SETTLE` 2.5 s; `BathPartnerOK` rejects anyone still bathing. SkyrimNet pack 0.7.0.

### 0.8 scene actors leave furniture (`ODS_Main` only; no new records)

- `LeaveFurniture(acts)` runs in `StartThread` (and before the rescue thread) before `OThreadBuilder.Create`: non-player actors with `GetSitState() != 0` or `GetSleepState() != 0` get `MoveTo(self)` + `EvaluatePackage()`, a bounded wait (16 x 0.25 s) for `GetSitState() == 0`, then one more nudge. Runs for bed scenes (`kFurn`) too; OStim seats them on the bed. Activating a victim (intervention) is unchanged. SkyrimNet pack 0.8.0.

### 0.9 Savior Complex and Killer Rabbit vs Vigilants (`ODS_Main`, `ODS_MCM`; new lists and one message)

Two independent wilderness-road modules, OFF by default, each with its own toggle and chance (`bSavior` / `fSaviorChance` 2.0, `bBunnyFight` / `fBunnyFightChance` 2.0). Kinds `K_SAVIOR` 16 and `K_BUNNYFIGHT` 17. They share `TravelWanted()` / `OnUpdateGameTime` / wait `RollKinds` with Travel & roads (checked first). `RoadEventPlaceOK()` requires outdoors, location category 5 (wilderness), no `LocTypeCity` / `LocTypeTown` in the location tree, and no unique essential/protected NPC within 4000 (`QuestNPCNearby`). Adults only (`IsMinor`). Panic / master off (`AbortAll` -> `ClearRoadEvent`) wakes anyone left unconscious and deletes spawns. No `ActorBase.SetEssential`. MCM `GetVersion` 8. SkyrimNet pack 0.9.0. 0.8 furniture fix is unchanged.

- **Savior Complex.** Spawned only: traveler from `ODS_PoolTraveler` (vanilla hunter `0x73FBE`, farmer templates `0xBD759` / `0xBD75E`, WE farmers `0x1034E4` / `0x1034E6`), bandit from `ODS_PoolBandit`. Needs `PairScene` for the pair. `StartEncounter` metadata `odangerous,aggressive,savior`. `StartText` / `DirectNarration` state plainly what the bandit is doing to the traveler (blunt wording on purpose, so the AI is not left to guess). `ODS_SaviorChoice.Show()`: 0 Watch, 1 Join in, 2 Rescue. Choice is also sent to SkyrimNet (`TalkNet`). Watch: scene continues, player is not added. Join: `bSaviorJoining` suppresses `Aftermath` while the first thread is stopped, then a 3-actor `SceneForSet` (bandit + player attacking the traveler); if none exists, it falls back to watch. Rescue (or `Intervene` / activate-or-hit while watching): stop the thread, bandit `StartCombat` on the player, `bRoadFight` + 1 s `OnUpdate` (`WatchRoadFight`). On bandit death, the traveler thanks the player via SkyrimNet (`SaviorVictory`). Victim health is topped up during the fight so they are not killed in the crossfire. Spawns go through `kSpawned` and the usual cleanup.
- **Killer Rabbit vs Vigilants.** Locked without `KillerBunnyOK()`. Two Vigilants from `ODS_PoolVigilant` (generic `0x10C44E`... templates from the UESP list; never Keeper Carcette `0xBFB55` or other unique Vigilants). ## 6. Known limits

- No hook for vanilla arrest dialogue; the stand-in clears the bounty after a jail scene with the player.
- Pickpocket and brawl escalation are shown as locked in the MCM, not built.
- A jail or barracks that shares a location with its town is only caught by sleeping or waiting there.
- ZaZ only changes preferred scenes plus narration. No ZaZ functions are called.
- Defeat of the player needs the optional "keep the player alive" setting, or they die normally. With ODefeat Reforged or Baka Defeat installed, player defeat is left to it.
- OStim Kidnapped Redux's events carry no actors, so kidnappers and taken followers are not named. Its night-roll handoff only knows ODangerousNights, so O'Deadly night attacks and its kidnappings can both happen at night.
- ODefeat Reforged third-party outcomes other than `SSLV Entry` are not narrated.
- Baka Defeat does not broadcast the knockdown, surrender or struggle; the defeat is first told when Baka's first scene with the player starts (or through its outcome event). Tied-up states are not narrated.
- Follower rescue starts OStim while combat continues nearby; other enemies can still hit the follower (kept from dying only through the temporary protected flag).
- iActions drunk tiers assume its default 2 points per level.
- Bathing in Skyrim's start event exists only for the player; followers are caught through its bathing effect.
- With Sacrosanct the Dark Gift turns the player at once (via `VampireChange`) rather than through the disease stage.
- Butterflies are vanilla critter activators driven by `TranslateTo`; the vanilla critter script may clean some up early.
- Savior Complex join-in needs a three-actor OStim scene for bandit + player + traveler; without one the player stays and watches.
- 1.0 Join in needs a three-actor (or, for a take-over, two-actor) scene for that exact cast. Only this mod's consensual cheating, shared-room and bathing scenes are covered; other mods' scenes are not.
- 1.0 werewolf spawns rely on the vanilla template `0x1E791` placing in `WerewolfBeastRace`; a spawn in any other race is discarded.
- 1.0 beast-form offers come a few seconds after the transformation; the vanilla beast-form timer keeps running during the scene.
- 1.0 Blood Born Pathogen: our werewolf change is a bare `SetRace` (no transformation idle); armor handling during it is the engine's. Vampires get no eyes; 1.1: their vampire lord form (SkyrimNet only) is a bare `SetRace` too. Growl followers are handed to Growl. At most 10 tracked NPCs.

## 7. Rebuild

From this repo, with Caprica (`-g skyrim`) and import folders for vanilla/SKSE, SkyUI SDK (`SKI_ConfigBase` must include `SetInputOptionValue`), OStim Standalone, powerofthree's Papyrus Extender and the SkyrimNet API. `compile.sh` adds `tools/stubs/` (compile-only stand-ins for `Dirty_BathingQuest`, `JValue`, `JArray`, `PlayerVampireQuestScript`, `DefeatOStimThread`; never ship them):

```
CAPRICA=/path/to/caprica IMPORTS=/path/to/import1:/path/to/import2 ./tools/compile.sh
python3 tools/scrub.py build/Scripts/*.pex
python3 tools/mkesp.py build/ODeadlySkyrim.esp
python3 tools/verify.py build/ODeadlySkyrim.esp
```

`scrub.py` strips compile paths and machine names from `.pex` headers (source basename, user `coronerra`, machine `-`).
